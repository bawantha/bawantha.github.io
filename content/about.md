I build software with a product mindset: fast interfaces, clean architecture, and systems that stay reliable when real users show up.

Based in Australia and currently working at Commonwealth Bank, I spend most of my time around mobile engineering, API design, and practical developer tooling. My strongest public work sits across Flutter, Android, Kotlin, Dart, and backend experiments that turn ideas into something people can actually use.

## What I Focus On

- Mobile product engineering with an eye for performance, polish, and maintainability.
- Thoughtful app architecture in Flutter, Jetpack Compose, Kotlin, and supporting backend services.
- Open-source tools that remove friction for other developers.
- Writing clear technical notes so hard-won lessons do not disappear after shipping.

## Things I Like Building

- Useful SDK wrappers and integration tools, especially in payment and app-platform workflows.
- Internal tools and developer experience improvements that save repetition.
- Product ideas with a clear user need, including current side work around `uteflow.com.au`.
- Personal experiments that connect mobile UX with reliable backend foundations.

## Beyond The Code

I also write at [bawantha.blog](https://bawantha.blog/), where I publish notes on software, interviews, career growth, and the small details that make engineering feel human. That mix of product work and writing shapes how I build: practical first, but always with care for clarity.
