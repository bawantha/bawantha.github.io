The best snapshot of my work is on [GitHub](https://github.com/bawantha), but these projects are a good starting point.

## payhere_flutter

[payhere_flutter](https://github.com/bawantha/payhere_flutter) is a Dart wrapper around the native PayHere Android SDK. It is the kind of work I enjoy most: bridging platform complexity into something cleaner and easier for app teams to adopt.

## flutter-widget-catcher

[flutter-widget-catcher](https://github.com/bawantha/flutter-widget-catcher) is a productivity tool for Flutter developers. It focuses on reducing friction during UI work, which says a lot about how I think about engineering: save time, make iteration lighter, and help developers stay in flow.

## composifi

[composifi](https://github.com/bawantha/composifi) is where I explored expressive motion and polished UI ideas with Jetpack Compose. It reflects the side of my work that cares about feel, not just function.

## ktor-banking

[ktor-banking](https://github.com/bawantha/ktor-banking) is a Kotlin backend experiment shaped around banking-style APIs. It shows my interest in dependable service design and the systems behind mobile experiences.

## Open-Source Contributions

I also contribute beyond my own repos, including work around [Apache Superset](https://github.com/apache/superset). I enjoy stepping into larger codebases, understanding how they move, and improving them without overcomplicating the result.
